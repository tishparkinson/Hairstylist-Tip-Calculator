The Fastest Mental Math Method

  1. Move the decimal one place left. $84.00 → $8.40. That's 10%.
  2. Double it. $8.40 × 2 = $16.80. That's 20%.

That's it. Two steps, no calculator needed.

Is 20% Always the Right Amount?

Twenty percent is the current standard for good service across most US personal service industries — restaurants, hair salons, rideshare, delivery, spas, and more. But the right amount for your situation also depends on where you live. In high cost-of-living cities, 20% is the floor. In lower-income areas, it may be slightly above expectation.
