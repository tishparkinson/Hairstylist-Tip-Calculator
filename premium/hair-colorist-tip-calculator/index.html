How much do you tip a hair colorist?

The standard tip for a hair colorist is 18–22% of the service cost. Color services take more time and skill than cuts, so 20–25% is appropriate for complex work like balayage or full highlights.

Do you tip the colorist and the shampoo person separately?

Yes — if a separate person shampooed and processed your hair, a small $5–$10 tip for them directly is appropriate, separate from the colorist's tip.

Do you tip more for a corrective color service?

Yes — corrective color is the most demanding and time-intensive service a colorist performs. 20–25% is appropriate for complex corrections.

Hair Colorist Tipping in the United States

Color services require a different tipping calculus than a simple haircut. Here's what's standard for every service type.

Balayage & Highlights

20% is the standard for any multi-step color service. Balayage involves freehand technique built on years of training — 20%–25% for exceptional work is well-regarded and increasingly common.

Color Correction

Color correction deserves a tip even when you're not fully satisfied. It's the most skill-intensive service in a salon, often requires multiple sessions, and involves significant product costs. 15%–20% is fair even for an imperfect result.

Unhappy With Results?

A disappointing color result doesn't mean no tip. 10%–15% acknowledges the time and effort while reflecting your experience. Speak to the salon manager about corrections — don't just withhold the tip and leave.

Your City Matters

In high cost-of-living cities, 20% is the floor expectation for color services. Our calculator uses US Census income data for your county so your suggestion reflects actual local norms.

Frequently Asked Questions — Hair Colorist Tips

How much to tip for color correction?

15%–20% is appropriate even for an imperfect result. Color correction is complex, rarely fully resolved in one session, and your colorist invested real skill attempting a difficult transformation. If you need a second appointment to complete the correction, 12%–15% at the first session is fair.

Should I tip if I hate my hair color?

Yes — 10%–15% is appropriate for genuinely disappointing color work. Complete zero-tip situations are very rare and reserved for negligent or harmful service. Instead of skipping the tip, speak with the salon manager about the result and what can be done to correct it.

How much to tip for balayage?

20% is the standard. On a $250 balayage, that's $50. For an exceptional result, 20%–25% is a meaningful acknowledgment of the freehand technique and multi-hour time investment.
