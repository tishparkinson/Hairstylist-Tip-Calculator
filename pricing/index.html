Common Questions

Why does location matter for wedding tips?
A 15% tip in Manhattan carries different weight than 15% in rural Idaho. Our calculators use US Census median household income data for your county — so the suggestion reflects what's actually fair where you live, not a national average that ignores your area.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. No contracts. Cancel from your Stripe billing portal in one click. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period.
What if I only need it for one month?
That's what it's built for. Subscribe the month before your wedding, use all 15 calculators, then cancel. $4.99 for complete confidence on every vendor tip.
Is my data stored or shared?
No. All calculations run entirely in your browser. We never see your city, your service details, or your tip amounts. Nothing is sent to a server.

No. The 78 free calculators are always available with no account or payment of any kind.
