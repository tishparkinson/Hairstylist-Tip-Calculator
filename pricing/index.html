Common Questions
Why does location matter?
A 15% tip in Manhattan carries different weight than 15% in rural Idaho. Our calculators use US Census median household income data for your county — so the suggestion reflects what's actually fair where you live.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel from your Subscription Portal in one click. Access continues until the end of your billing period.
What if I only need it for one month?
That's what it's built for. Subscribe, use all 23 calculators, then cancel. $4.99 for complete confidence on every tip.
Is my data stored?
No. All calculations run in your browser. We never see your city, your inputs, or your tip amounts.
